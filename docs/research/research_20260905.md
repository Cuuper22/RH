## The existing signed-pair predicate is false

In `RH/Zeta85/Arith.lean`, `SignedPairTraceGrade sigma` independently
quantifies `IV`, although the accompanying text calls it the integral of V.
For every sigma>1, choose w=V=1 and IV=0. These choices satisfy the literal
boundedness hypotheses. The unspecified singular series disappears.

Here is a proof needing only Chebyshev bounds. Put X=T^sigma,
H=T^(sigma-1), M=floor(2X), and m=floor(2H). Partition the integers
1,...,M into J=ceil(M/m) consecutive blocks of length at most m. With
a_n=Lambda(n), the positive-shift aggregate contains every unordered pair
of different integers in the same block. Therefore

\[
 \sum_{1\le h\le\lceil2H\rceil}
 \sum_{1\le n\le\lceil2X\rceil}\Lambda(n)\Lambda(n+h)
 \ge\frac12\left\{\frac{\psi(M)^2}{J}
                         -\sum_{n\le M}\Lambda(n)^2\right\}.
\]

Chebyshev gives psi(M)>>M and psi(M)<<M; also
sum Lambda(n)^2 <= log(M) psi(M). Since J=O(X/H) and H/log X tends
to infinity, the right side is >>XH. It contradicts the asserted
K X(log T)^(-A) bound for any A>0, independently of S.

Thus `signedPair_traceGrade_lt_5_4`, together with its already proved
`BBLRErrorBound` premise, assumes a false statement. The current legacy
axiom layer is mathematically inconsistent, not just awaiting a plausible
estimate. This refutation has not been formalized in Lean. The unconditional
`Zeta23` import tree is separate. The definitions and compiled dependency
lists are retained for provenance; no result here uses those axioms.

Repairing this requires the actual source weight and its tied main term.
Changing only the desired logarithmic exponent cannot repair IV=0.

## A useful arithmetic estimate and a smaller target

[Complete Möbius energy](mobius_energy_20260905.md) proves, for
a=tau/ell and ell comparable to log Z,

\[
 \sum_{n\le Z}\left|\sum_{d\mid n}\mu(d)d^{ia}\log d\right|^2
       \ll (2+|\tau|)^C Z\log Z.
\]

The proof preserves the complete Euler product, applies a uniform
nonnegative multiplicative mean theorem on a small complex circle, and
uses Cauchy's derivative formula. It removes the extra log log factor in
a direct differentiated-product bound. The note also proves the exact
complementary-divisor identity for every n>1, including nonsquarefree n:

\[
 \Lambda_R(n)-\Lambda(n)
   =\mu(\operatorname{rad}n)
                 \Lambda_{\operatorname{rad}(n)/R}(\operatorname{rad}n).
\]

The raw second-trace scale is Q_T=T ell^3/(2pi). At support 1.49 the
normalized cosine profile has diagonal cost 1.14724435049..., so the
actual off-diagonal plus all secondary errors may be as large as
0.00275564950... Q_T and still permit 85%. This is a one-sided,
window-specific target; a full saturation asymptotic is unnecessary.

The estimate above applies to complete divisor coefficients. Internal
factor cutoffs can prevent that collapse. At dual scale x~1/R, Z~TR,
Parseval alone also loses a factor R. Neither problem has been removed.

## The corrected single-plateau quartic class cannot certify 85%

This is a certificate-class obstruction, not a barrier to the research goal.
Grant a normalized nonnegative full profile u on an interval of width one,
its cost D=int u^2+int int |x-y|u(x)u(y), and one localized plateau
q=m 1_[−a/2,a/2] <=u, where 0<a<=1/2 and am<=1.
Grant the block's four proposed raw moments, divided by the total count:

\[
 ma,\quad m^2(a+a^3/3),\quad m^3(a+a^3),\quad
 m^4(a+2a^3+4a^5/15).
\]

They have a feasible spectral law of total mass a: eigenvalue m(1+aZ),
where Z has masses 5/24,7/12,5/24 at -2/sqrt(5),0,2/sqrt(5).
At a candidate simple fraction 17/20, delete mass 3/40 from the largest
eigenvalues, as the existing stability inequality permits. Write T(a,m)
for the remaining integral of (eigenvalue−1)_+^2.

The bandwidth-one variational bound gives D>=13/10. When m>=1,
Jensen and u=q+r additionally give

\[
 D\ge\max\left\{\frac{13}{10},\,
 1+\frac{a(m-1)^2}{1-a}+\frac{m^2a^3}{3}
                         +\frac{(1-am)ma^2}{2}\right\}=:D_0.
\]

The cross term follows from min_y int_[−a/2,a/2]|x-y|dx=a^2/4.
The [exact rational verifier](../../verify/scaled_quartic_obstruction.py)
proves T(a,m)<D_0−23/20 on 3/40<=a<=1/2, 1<=m<=1/a.
It closes 192 rational boxes, discards 15 infeasible boxes, and has smallest
strict certified margin 4652388417683/14889792621772800.
For a<=3/40 the whole block is trimmed. For m<=1 its full positive tail
is at most a^3/6<=1/48, and 13/10−1/48>23/20.

Thus these isolated moments, the baseline cost, and the existing scalar
tail inequality cannot certify 85%, even after allowing arbitrary mean
and optimizing the full profile. The feasible law is not asserted to be
a zeta spectrum or a jointly feasible full/block configuration.

## Analytic transfer and further statistics

[Ordered moment transfer](ordered_moment_20260905.md) records the
complex-zero feature operator and the concentrating-height derivation
from the unconditional smooth Rudnick--Sarnak formula. It also records a
specific failed shortcut: the feature space does not supply a block
dimension aN. Centered quartic polynomials cannot charge that invented
dimension. Raw polynomials with p(0)=0 avoid zero-padding dependence.

A separate cubic-only shortcut is also false for actual analytic kernels.
For K(z)=sin(pi z)/(pi z), take four points ±iy and d±iy. If
A=K(d), B=K(2iy), W=K(d+2iy), direct multiplication gives

\[
 \operatorname{tr}G^2=4+4(A^2+B^2+\Re W^2),\qquad
 \operatorname{tr}G(G-I)(G-2I)=24AB\Re W.
\]

For d=1/2+(1+1/sqrt(2))/(4pi y), their ratio is
−(12sqrt(2)/pi)y+O(1). Negative cubic defect is therefore not controlled
by a constant times the second trace, even for genuine conjugate-pair
evaluation geometry. These four points are a counterexample to that
inequality, not asserted zeros of zeta.

The resulting escape retains a positive ordered, endpoint-reaching fourth
moment. It yields the comparison inequality and small improvement in
[§§6--8](ordered_moment_20260905.md). The resolvent identity in §9 is a
concrete higher-order continuation, with its support and limit problems
recorded. No 85% certificate has been obtained from it.

Two other attacks were retired in this pass. Shift-first Poisson duality
preserves the four Möbius signs and improves the subordinate BBLR exponent
from 1.61 to 1.51, but leaves the dominant 1.79 unchanged; the transform
is an involution, so repeating it supplies no further gain. The natural
real Möbius family of reciprocal logarithmic derivatives has diagonal
density at least the zeta density |alpha|, while its valid signature
certificate requires cost <=40/37 for 85%; the bandwidth-one cost floor
1.327499... exceeds that threshold. An unmarked derivative-zero count or
that reciprocal two-trace family does not close the original-zero target.
