# Research toward a higher unconditional proportion, 2026-10-03

Starting frontier: `2 - D(u) + 1/271803 = 0.6725043820976` simple zeros on
the critical line ([sharpened cubic note](sharpened_cubic_gain_20260905.md)).
This pass ran six independent research lines and one referee audit. Status
labels follow the notes: PROVED means a written proof, CHECKED means an
exact rational or interval verifier. Nothing here is machine-checked in Lean.

## New frontier: 0.6725162800

[Localized cubic certificate](multiwindow_20261003.md): zero slack forces
`p(G) = G^3 - 3G^2 + 2G = 0` as an operator, so every diagonally weighted
trace `tr(p(G) M_phi)` must vanish, while the cubic defect density changes
sign across the window. The sharpened comparison extends to diagonal
multipliers through `||W Phi W||_2^2 <= ||phi||^2 ||W||_4^4` and
`||W||_4^4 = tr(H^2 K^2)/2 <= tr H^3/3`. An exact rational degree-20 weight
on the Taylor-8 cosine profile gives

    liminf N_0^s/N >= 2 - D(u) + 1/64200 = 0.6725162800034...

The [referee audit](weighted_transfer_audit_20261003.md) re-derived the
weighted cubic and ordered fourth limits independently. A vertex weight
multiplies the difference-variable symbol pointwise, so Fourier support is
unchanged; the inherited derivation already proves the general-symbol limit.
All collapse coefficients, operator lemmas and certificate inputs agree.
Verdict: PROVED with the same status as the previous frontier. The
[marked triple certificate](triple_correlation_20261003.md) is a weaker
instance of the same idea (1/216600), also PROVED.

## The largest open lever: inequality (**)

[Positivity class](positivity_class_20261003.md): the Cheer--Goldston
mechanism does not transfer by positivity alone, since a lone off-line pair
breaks every test with negative mass beyond bandwidth one. Its full content
is one inequality about conjugation-invariant point sets,

    Sigma_Z(rho) >= kappa (2N - s_1),

with `rho` the bandwidth-one part of the Cheer--Goldston LP test. If it
holds, the unconditional proportion becomes 0.67908; any `kappa' > 0.99504`
already beats the frontier. Proved for real sets, lone pairs and
common-height pairs; CONJECTURE in general.

The cell-based joint-field inequality (multi-height memo, Conjecture 3.2)
is REFUTED by spacing-2 pair lattices at height 0.5 (damage exceeds energy
by 7-14 %), although (**) itself holds there with ratio at least 1.46. The
component-local replacement, Conjecture 3.2', provably implies the
common-height case and survives 236 test configurations with worst ratio
0.87 ([joint-field memo](complex_cg_jointfield_20261003.md)). A continuum
heuristic warns that every reduction discarding the off-band term may fail
above height about 70.

Conditional corollary (PROVED): if every zero with `T < gamma <= 2T`
satisfies `|beta - 1/2| <= 1/(2 log T)`, at least 0.67892 of zeros are
simple and on the line; the published bound under that hypothesis is 0.617.

Refuted along the way: positive-kernel certificates, termwise height
domination, a high-height threshold, Lemma R splits, and common-shift
monotonicity. [Adversarial searches](complex_cg_adversary_20261003.md),
about 31,000 optimisations including middle heights, found no violation;
the tightest configurations are real lattices of doubles (ratio 1.00025)
and common-height pair lattices (ratio 1.00444). Allowing signed band-one
certificates does not help: the real-case LP already allows signs, and
pair lattices are not binding.

## Priced but blocked

[Support extension](support_epsilon_20261003.md): a one-sided bound
`F <= 1` on `1 < |alpha| <= 1 + eps` gains linearly with slope
`csc^2(1/sqrt 2)/2 - 1/2 = 0.68476` (eps = 0.001 gives 0.67318). Any fixed
bounded `F <= C` on a fixed strip gains about `0.3/C`. The needed input is a
bounded-constant variance for primes in intervals up to `T^tau`, still open.

## Closed routes

- [Mollified discrete moments](mollified_discrete_20261003.md): RH enters
  CGG only through `S2 = sum |B zeta'(rho)|^2`; one off-line pair with values
  `(a, -a)` reproduces both moments, so count bounds cannot rescue it.
- [xi-prime bridge](xiprime_bridge_20261003.md): `2 Omega = s - N_0^s + G_d +
  delta` exactly, so the wrong-extremum bound is the target in disguise.
- [Triple correlations](triple_correlation_20261003.md): Rudnick--Sarnak
  frequencies sum to zero, so no single frequency reaches 1; triple data in
  that range is mock-Gaussian and adds nothing beyond the localized cubic.
- [Multi-window second order](multiwindow_20261003.md): stacked windows
  reduce to one mixture window under Lemma R.
